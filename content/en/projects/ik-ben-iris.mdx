# Ik ben Iris

A multi-tenant platform for finding and managing medical aids, tracking statuses, and providing overview dashboards for healthcare professionals and administrators.

More info: [ikbeniris.com](https://www.ikbeniris.com) · [alltrackmedical.nl](https://www.alltrackmedical.nl)

## What I did

- Multi-tenant architecture for multiple organisations
- Search and management functionality for medical aids
- Status overviews and admin interfaces
