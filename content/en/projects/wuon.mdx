# WUON

WUON (Weefsel Uitname Organisatie Nederland) coordinates the procurement of donor tissue — including corneas, bone, skin, and heart valves — from deceased donors. The application supports healthcare professionals at every step of this sensitive, strictly regulated process.

This includes registering donor data, screening for eligibility, coordinating procurement teams, documenting each step, and ensuring full traceability. The focus is on clarity, error prevention, and speed of use.

More information about the organization: [wuon.org](https://wuon.org)

## What I did

- Built complex form-heavy flows for healthcare professionals
- Process support with conditional steps, validation logic, and status management
- Designed interfaces focused on clarity and error prevention
- Collaborated with stakeholders and end users for continuous improvement
- Built an inventory management system for medical supplies used in procurement procedures
