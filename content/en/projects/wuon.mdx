# WUON

An application that supports the donor explantation process. Healthcare professionals follow structured workflows and forms around this sensitive and precise process. This includes registering donor data, coordinating teams, and documenting each step. The focus is on clarity, error prevention, and speed of use.

More information about the organization: [wuon.org](https://wuon.org)

## What I did

- Built form-heavy flows for healthcare professionals
- Implemented process support with conditional steps and status management
- Focused on clarity, error prevention, and speed of use
- Collaborated with stakeholders for continuous improvement
- Built an inventory management system for medical supplies
