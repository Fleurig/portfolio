# Pillr

A B2B platform for construction teams to manage, share, and collaborate on 3D models. Multi-tenant, scalable, and used in multiple large construction projects.

Built from the ground up together with two colleagues.

## What I did

- Front-end development in Vue.js
- Back-end development in C# ASP.NET Core
- Multi-tenant architecture and authorization logic
- Charting and 3D visualization of construction models
- Closely collaborated within a small, self-managing team with a high level of responsibility
