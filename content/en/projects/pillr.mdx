# Pillr

A B2B platform for construction teams to manage, share, and collaborate on 3D models. Multi-tenant, scalable, and deployed across multiple large construction projects.

Built from the ground up together with two colleagues. I was responsible for around 50% of the front-end and contributed to the back-end as well.

## What I did

- Front-end development in Vue.js (~50% of the front-end)
- Back-end contributions in C#/.NET
- Multi-tenant architecture and authorisation logic
- Close collaboration in a small, autonomous team with high ownership
