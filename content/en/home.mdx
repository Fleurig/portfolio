I'm a front-end developer based in **Haarlem** with over 6 years of professional experience in React, Next.js, and TypeScript. I have a strong preference for building **design systems** — not as an afterthought, but as a foundation. Reusable components, well-considered APIs, and consistency across products: that's what gets me excited.

At **Improvement-IT** I'm responsible for all front-end concerns: from architecture and AWS Amplify to component libraries in Storybook. Before that, I worked at **CAD & Company** on Pillr, a complex B2B platform for 3D models, where I contributed to both the front-end (Vue.js) and back-end (C#) in a close-knit team.
