# Pillr

Pillr is a cloud-based BIM platform for construction teams to manage 3D models, collaborate across disciplines, and coordinate building processes. The platform covers the full construction lifecycle: model validation, task management, data exports, and multi-party collaboration. Multi-tenant, scalable, and deployed across multiple large construction projects.

Built from the ground up with two colleagues.

## What I did

- Front-end development in Vue.js: component library, routing, state management
- Back-end development in C# ASP.NET Core: REST APIs, authorization logic, data layer
- Multi-tenant architecture: per-client isolation, role-based access control
- 3D BIM viewer: web-based display and navigation of IFC models
- Charting and dashboard components for construction data and progress insights
- Worked in a small, self-managing team with high autonomy and direct end-to-end responsibility
