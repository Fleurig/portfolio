# Ik ben Iris

Ik ben Iris® is a logistics platform for managing medical equipment in healthcare institutions. Using smart sensors (BLE, RFID, GPS), it provides real-time visibility into the location and status of devices — from IV pumps and wheelchairs to patient lifts.

The platform is developed by Alltrack Medical in collaboration with Improvement-IT, and is used by hospitals and care organisations across the Netherlands and Belgium.

More info: [ikbeniris.com](https://www.ikbeniris.com) · [alltrackmedical.nl](https://www.alltrackmedical.nl/ik-ben-iris)

## What I did

- Multi-tenant architecture for multiple healthcare organisations
- Real-time search and management functionality for medical equipment
- Status overviews and admin interfaces for care staff and administrators
- Dynamic forms for equipment registration and management
- Integration with external systems (including TOPdesk, Ultimo)
- Contributed to the mobile app in React Native
